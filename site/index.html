Every dollar here is simulated. There are no brokerage credentials in this project and no code path that can place a real order.

v2 runs a cross-sectional long/short ranker on the NautilusTrader engine: it ranks ~670 liquid US stocks every 30 minutes, buys the strongest and shorts the weakest, sizes each by its own volatility, and holds overnight. A weekend news job tilts the ranking. FINDINGS.md · v1 archive
